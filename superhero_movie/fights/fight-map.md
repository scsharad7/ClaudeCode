- **Shots** are final, kept shots (average 2–3 s each), not generations. Expect about 8–15 generations per kept shot.
- **Seconds of fighting** is the action on screen, not counting setup and aftermath.
- **Beats needing motion reference:** list only beats with specific choreography or the signature move. Power blasts and launches usually don't need one.
- **AI failure risk:**
  - **Low:** powers, launches, debris, one character per shot.
  - **Med:** fast limbs, two fighters in a wide, a tricky camera move.
  - **High:** contact, grappling, hands in close-up, fighters close together. Redesign high-risk beats before generating.
