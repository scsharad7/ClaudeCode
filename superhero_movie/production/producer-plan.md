## 1. Short-tier decision framework

Pick the tier you can **actually fund today**, not the one you hope to raise.

| | **Lean (~$5k)** | **Target (~$15k)** | **Ideal (~$30k)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shoot days | 2 | 3–4 | 4–5 |
| Fight length | 1–2 min | 2–3 min | 3–4 min |
| Stunt team | Coordinator + 2 performers | Coordinator + 3–4 performers | Coordinator + 4–6, simple rigging (pro riggers) |
| Hero suits | 1 hero + 1 stunt | 2 (hero, stunt) | 3 (hero, stunt, damage) |
| VFX shots | ~5–10 accents | ~10–25 | ~25–40 |
| Crew | Mostly deferred, key heads paid | Small paid core | Paid core of 10–12 |
| Cast | Non-union, or SAG-AFTRA **Short Project** (not Micro, which bans stunts) | Short Project | Short Project (caps at $50k) |

**Ring-fenced at every tier (never cut):** stunt coordinator on every action day, rehearsal days, a medic, and insurance that declares the stunts. If money runs short, cut a set piece or a VFX shot instead.

**Rule of thumb:** each minute of nonstop fighting needs 2–3 shoot days. Fight length drives the tier.

## 4. Financing plan for the short

| Option | Speed | Control | Fits tier | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Self-fund | Fast | Full | Lean | Never use debt you can't repay |
| Friends & family | Medium | High | Lean–Target | Written terms via LLC; securities laws apply |
| Crowdfunding (Kickstarter, Indiegogo, Seed&Spark) | Slow (needs ~6 weeks of prep) | High | Target–Ideal | ~10–15% to fees and rewards; ties you to online-first release |
| Grants / fiscal sponsorship | Slow | High | Any | Most favour social-issue work over genre |
| In-kind (gear, locations, food) | Medium | High | Any | Get it in writing |
| Private equity | Slow | Lower | Feature only | Not worth it for a short |

**Recommendation:** fund the Lean tier yourself or with friends and family so the shoot never depends on a campaign. Crowdfund the top-up to Target or Ideal only if you choose **online-first** at week 8. Details: [production-roadmap.md §6](../research/production-roadmap.md#6-funding-options).

## 5. Top-8 risk register

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Injury on a fight day | Med | Severe | Coordinator on every action day, rehearsal, pads, medic, stunts declared to the insurer |
| 2 | Scope creep (fight grows past the budget) | High | High | Tier locked at week 3; any new beat must name what it replaces |
| 3 | `00-my-idea.md` stays blank and writing stalls | High | High | Week 1 is your only task; rough notes count |
| 4 | VFX overrun | High | High | Powers cheap to show; VFX lead counts the shots before the script locks |
| 5 | Unclean chain of title, or IP resemblance | Med | Severe | LLC, deal memos with rights assignment, originality check, title report |
| 6 | Suit late or can't move in a fight | Med | High | Costume designer hired by week 10; movement test with the stunt team |
| 7 | Release route chosen too late (short locked out of festivals) | Med | High | Decide by week 8; post nothing publicly until then |
| 8 | Unpaid crew drop out | Med | Med | Pay something; signed deal memos; a backup name for each key role |

## 6. Day-90 checkpoint (end of week 13)

**Go** to shoot planning only if all of these are true:

- [ ] Feature draft 2 is finished; the short script is locked
- [ ] Core team of 4–6 is committed with signed deal memos
- [ ] LLC formed; lawyer consult done; copyright registered
- [ ] Short budgeted at a chosen tier and funded, or the campaign is ready to launch
- [ ] Shoot date set 6–10 weeks out
- [ ] Insurance quotes **declare the stunts**
- [ ] Release route decided

**If not ready:** drop one tier, or move the date. Never cut rehearsal or safety.
