## 1. Short-tier decision framework

Pick the tier you can **actually fund today**, not the one you hope to raise. The short's budget range is $5k–$30k.

**Numbers live in one place.** Shoot days, stunt performers, VFX shot counts, suits, crew size and totals for each tier are in [budget.md §1](budget.md#1-assumptions-edit-these-first). That file wins wherever it disagrees with this one. The headline shape:

| | **Lean (~$5k)** | **Target (~$15k)** | **Ideal (~$30k)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fight sequence (on-screen length) | ~2 min | ~3 min | 3–4 min |
| Nonstop fighting (continuous contact) | 45–60 s | ~90 s | ~2 min |
| Shoot days (fight + drama) | 2 (2 + 0) | 4 (3 + 1) | 5 (4 + 1) |
| Stunt performers | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| VFX shots | ~3–5 | ~8–12 | ~15–25 |
| Cast | Non-union | Non-union | Non-union, or a union short (SAG-AFTRA Short Project) |

**A union short means the Ideal tier at minimum.** The SAG-AFTRA Micro agreement bans stunts, so a union short uses the Short Project agreement, which caps at $50k. Confirm its terms with SAGindie.

**Ring-fenced at every tier (never cut):** stunt coordinator on every action day, rehearsal days, a medic, and insurance that declares the stunts. If money runs short, cut a set piece or a VFX shot instead.

**Rule of thumb:** each minute of nonstop fighting needs 2–3 shoot days, and each extra ~30 seconds adds about one fight day. Fight length drives the tier.

## 4. Financing plan for the short

| Option | Speed | Control | Fits tier | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Self-fund | Fast | Full | Lean | Never use debt you can't repay |
| Friends & family | Medium | High | Lean–Target | Before week 9, gifts or pledges only. Profit-sharing money only after the LLC exists and a lawyer has drafted the documents. Securities laws apply |
| Crowdfunding (Kickstarter, Indiegogo, Seed&Spark) | Slow (~6 weeks of prep) | High | Target–Ideal | ~10–15% to fees and rewards. Money arrives ~2 weeks after the campaign closes, so don't book deposits against it |
| Grants / fiscal sponsorship (a nonprofit that receives donations for you, making them tax-deductible) | Slow | High | Any | Most grants favour social-issue work over genre |
| In-kind (donated gear, locations, food) | Medium | High | Any | Get it in writing |
| Private equity | Slow | Lower | Feature only | Not worth it for a short |

**Crowdfunding works on either release route.** A campaign to *make* the short doesn't cost festival eligibility. Only a public release of the finished short, or of the full fight, does that.

**Recommendation:** fund the Lean tier yourself or with gifts and pledges, so the shoot never depends on a campaign. Use crowdfunding to reach Target or Ideal, and schedule it so the money lands before deposits are due. Details: [production-roadmap.md §6](../research/production-roadmap.md#6-funding-options).

## 5. Top-8 risk register

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Injury on a fight day | Med | Severe | Coordinator on every action day, rehearsal, rated crash mats, medic, stunts declared to the insurer |
| 2 | Scope creep (fight grows past the budget) | High | High | Tier locked at week 4; any new beat must name what it replaces |
| 3 | `00-my-idea.md` stays blank and writing stalls | High | High | Week 1 is your only task; rough notes count |
| 4 | VFX overrun | High | High | Powers cheap to show; VFX lead counts the shots before the script locks |
| 5 | Unclean chain of title (the paper trail proving the LLC owns the film), or IP resemblance | Med | Severe | LLC, deal memos with rights assignment, originality check, title report |
| 6 | Insurance with stunts declared costs more than the tier allows | Med | High | Indicative quote before locking the tier (week 3) |
| 7 | Release route chosen too late (short locked out of festivals) | Med | High | Decide by week 8. Until then, post nothing of the finished short or the full fight; behind-the-scenes posts are fine |
| 8 | Unpaid crew drop out | Med | Med | Pay something; signed deal memos; a backup name for each key role |

## 6. Day-90 checkpoint (end of week 13)

**Go** to shoot planning only if all of these are true:

- [ ] Feature draft 2 is finished; the short script is locked
- [ ] Core team of 4–6 is committed with signed deal memos
- [ ] LLC formed; lawyer consult done; copyright registered
- [ ] Short budgeted at a chosen tier and funded, or the campaign is ready to launch
- [ ] Shoot date set 6–10 weeks out
- [ ] Insurance quotes **declare the stunts**
- [ ] Release route decided

**If not ready:** drop one tier, or move the date. Never cut rehearsal or safety.
