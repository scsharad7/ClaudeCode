How it works

From raw American frustration to a founder-ready shortlist, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Listen everywhere

    AI scrapes X and Reddit, a Typeform-style survey collects thousands of responses, and we have on-the-ground conversations in major US cities — from Brooklyn to the Bay to the Sun Belt.

  2. 02

    Score with the Itch Index

    Every problem is rated on Severity, Frequency, Market Whitespace, and TAM, then blended into a single Itch Score so the biggest opportunities rise to the top.

  3. 03

    Surface build-worthy problems

    We filter noise from signal, dedupe the 40,000+ raw inputs, and keep the itches with real severity, real frequency, and real room for a new company.

  4. 04

    Founders build

    Browse, filter, and sort. Pick an itch that keeps you up at night — and go scratch it for millions of Americans.

The Itch Index

A proprietary score that turns a messy complaint into a rankable opportunity. Four dimensions, one number from 55 to 100.

🔥

Severity

How much does this problem hurt when it hits? A surprise medical bill stings more than a slow app.

Scale 4–10 · weight 30%
🔁

Frequency

How often does it bite? Daily delivery fees beat a once-a-decade mortgage headache on volume.

Scale 2–10 · weight 25%
🕳️

Market Whitespace

How underserved is it today? A crowded category scores low; a wide-open one scores high.

Scale 4–9 · weight 20%
💵

TAM

How big is the US total addressable market in dollars and people? Bigger prize, bigger itch.

Scale 6–10 · weight 25%
Severity ×30% + Frequency ×25% + Whitespace ×20% + TAM ×25% = Itch Score
